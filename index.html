A little introduction

More than a place on the map.

Bangladesh is a landscape shaped by water, warmth and movement. Rivers thread through fertile plains, tea fields climb gentle slopes, and fishing boats return to shore beneath wide evening skies.

This guide brings together some of the places that make the country memorable — with ideas to help you explore thoughtfully and at your own pace.
