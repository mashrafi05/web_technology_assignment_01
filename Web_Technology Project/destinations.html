A gentle reminder

Let the place set the pace.

Some places are best enjoyed slowly. Leave time for local food, respectful conversations and the landscapes between the landmarks.

  • Check local travel guidance before setting out.
  • Choose responsible local operators where possible.
  • Carry reusable essentials and leave no litter behind.
