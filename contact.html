A few helpful notes

Before you set off.

Are the travel package prices real?

No. The package names, durations and prices are sample content for this website project, not live quotes or confirmed bookings.

Can I use this website to book a trip?

No. This is an informational front-end demonstration. It does not connect to a booking service or payment system.

Are the photographs from Bangladesh?

The photographs are illustrative travel images from Unsplash and may not depict the exact named location. Replace them with properly licensed, destination-specific images for a published version.

Does the contact form send messages?

No. It demonstrates HTML form elements and browser-level required-field and email-format checks. A real submission workflow needs a backend or a form service.
